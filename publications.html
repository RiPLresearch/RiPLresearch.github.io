Publication

Investigation of stress hormones across multiday seizure cycles

Stirling et al. · Brain Communications (2026)

Saliva cortisol tracked across predicted high- and low-risk periods shows hormone levels rise at the peak of a person's multiday seizure cycle.

Publication

Multiday cycles of heart rate are associated with seizure likelihood

An observational cohort study · eBioMedicine (2021)

Wearable heart-rate data reveals circadian, weekly and monthly cycles in nearly all participants, with seizures phase-locked to these cycles in many people with epilepsy.
